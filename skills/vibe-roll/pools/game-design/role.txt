# about: a special role or condition given to one player
lantern bearer: sees the true doors but cannot speak
key carrier: cannot jump or run
the marked: draws every enemy's attention
the bound: chained to another player
the blind: sees nothing but hears everything
the mute: can only use gestures
the timekeeper: alone can see the timer
the cartographer: alone can see the map
the conductor: sets the rhythm everyone must follow
the anchor: if they move, the floor drops
the decoy: looks like the boss to enemies
the healer who cannot heal themselves
the ghost: can pass walls but cannot touch objects
the bomb: explodes if they stand still too long
the judge: decides who gets the reward
the traitor: secretly works for the other side
the scribe: must write down every clue
the mirror: copies another player's moves
the battery: powers the machine by standing on it
the lightning rod: absorbs one hazard per round
the translator: alone can read the symbols
the runner: the only one who can leave the room
the shield: blocks damage for others but not themselves
the gambler: rolls a die every round for a random effect
the cursed: grows stronger and slower
the hostage: cannot act until rescued
the host's favorite: gets bonus points and enemies
the clockwinder: must keep a machine wound
the singer: must sing to keep the bridge up
the counter: must count out loud without stopping
