# about: the 24 Elder Futhark runes with their traditional sense
fehu: cattle, movable wealth
uruz: the aurochs, raw strength
thurisaz: the giant, a thorn
ansuz: a god, the spoken word
raidho: the ride, a journey
kenaz: the torch, knowledge
gebo: a gift and its obligation
wunjo: joy, belonging
hagalaz: hail, sudden disruption
naudiz: need, constraint
isa: ice, standstill
jera: the year, a harvest earned
eihwaz: the yew, endurance
perthro: the lot cup, chance
algiz: the elk, protection
sowilo: the sun, success
tiwaz: the sky god, justice and sacrifice
berkano: the birch, new growth
ehwaz: the horse, partnership
mannaz: humankind
laguz: water, the unconscious
ingwaz: the seed, potential
dagaz: daybreak, transformation
othala: inherited land, home
