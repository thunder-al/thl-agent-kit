# about: a turn in the plot, after Propp's folktale functions
someone leaves home
a warning is given
the warning is ignored
the villain scouts
the villain learns something they should not
the villain sets a trap
the victim is fooled
harm is done, or something precious goes missing
the trouble becomes known and someone is sent
the hero decides to act
the hero sets out
the hero is tested
the hero answers the test
the hero receives a gift or a helper
the hero is guided to the place
hero and villain meet in a fight
the hero is marked or wounded
the villain is defeated
the harm is undone
the hero turns back
the hero is pursued
the hero escapes
the hero arrives home unrecognized
a false hero makes claims
an impossible task is set
the task is done
the hero is recognized
the false hero is exposed
the hero is transformed
the villain is punished
the hero is rewarded
